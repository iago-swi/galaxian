# Suncrest changelog

What changed in each version of Suncrest, newest first. The version number is shown
on the game's title screen. Each version is also a
[release on GitHub](https://github.com/iago-swi/phoenix/releases?q=Suncrest), with the
standalone game of that version attached. The numbering rules are in
[VERSIONING.md](../VERSIONING.md).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-28)

First version.

- A fixed-screen bird shooter styled after sunlit Edo woodblock prints and gold-leaf
  scrolls, with five distinct sky palettes across each cycle.
- Five-stage repeating cycles modeled on the 1980 arcade game *Phoenix*: Dawn Flock,
  Gale Flock, Porcelain Brood, Crimson Brood and The Sun-Ark, growing faster and
  tougher each cycle.
- Three diving bird species in formation (Azure Swifts, Jade Kingfishers and Vermilion
  Kites), with double points for mid-dive hits and a rapid-kill flow multiplier up to
  ×4.
- Kintsugi porcelain eggs that weave across the sky and crack open into Great Solar
  Phoenixes; only body shots destroy them, while shot-off wings regrow after 3.6
  seconds.
- The Sun-Ark, a screen-wide descending pagoda fortress defended by three rows of
  destructible lacquer armour tiles, a horizontally revolving bronze shield belt, twin
  lantern turrets and escort birds.
- The Solar Ward force shield (`↓`, `S`, `Shift`, `X`, right click or touch **WARD**
  button) that blocks shots and destroys ramming small birds for 2 seconds before a
  5-second recharge, with clear ready indicators on the HUD and ship.
- Rare Twin Lance lantern power-ups dropped by Great Solar Phoenixes and diving birds.
- Three starting ships, extra ships as your score climbs (at 10,000 and every 20,000
  points after), and your best score saved in the browser.
- Synthesized pentatonic koto, taiko, flute and woodblock music and sound effects via
  Web Audio, with a mute key (`M`) and pause (`P` / `Esc`).
- Keyboard, mouse and mobile touch-screen controls.
- `Suncrest.html`, the complete game in a single file for playing offline.
