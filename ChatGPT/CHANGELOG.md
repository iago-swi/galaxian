# Sunspindle changelog

What changed in each version of Sunspindle, newest first. The version appears on the title screen. See the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/iago-swi/phoenix/releases?q=Sunspindle) and the [repository's numbering rules](../VERSIONING.md).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-28)

First version.

### Added

- Five stages over a sunlit mountain lake. Two formations of metal swifts give way to two broods of eggs and the descending Solar Press.
- Diving swifts fire at your ship and score double. Eggs can be broken early or left to hatch into kites with wings that grow back.
- Three rows of brass armour protect the press. Each direct hit sends its spindle sliding to a new position while escorts continue to dive.
- A force shield gives you 1.45 seconds of cover and takes five more seconds to recharge. You can move and fire under cover.
- Three ships at launch, another every 4,000 points, and a best score saved in your browser. Clearing the press starts a harder cycle.
- Keyboard and touch controls, pause, mute, and an original bell-and-bass soundtrack.
