Galaxian

An arcade game inspired by the 1979 original. A swarm holds formation at the top of the screen, and one attacker after another breaks away to dive at your ship. The version below was built by Claude; versions by other AI assistants, from the same brief, will join it here.

Nightflock

Built with Claude. A flock of neon-edged birds holds formation in the night sky, and swallows, gulls, falcons and ravens dive at your ship, each in its own way. Shoot a bird in mid-dive for double points, chain kills to multiply the score, and catch twin shots, rapid fire or a shield. Drawn with WebGL 2, with music and sound made in the browser.
