Phoenix

An arcade game inspired by the 1980 original. Birds dive at your ship, eggs hatch into larger birds whose wings grow back, and after a few waves a ship as wide as the screen comes down. A force shield, raised at the right moment, keeps you alive. Choose between three games built from the same brief by Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT.

Nightflock

Built with Claude. Flocks of swallows, gulls and falcons, drawn like the plates of a field guide, dive at your ship at dusk, eggs hatch into great ravens that must be hit between the wings, and every fourth wave brings the Aerie, a giant ship with one red eye. Raise the force shield at the right moment, shoot a bird in mid-dive for double points and chain kills to multiply the score. Drawn with WebGL 2, with music and sound made in the browser.

Play NightflockVersion 2.1.0

Suncrest

Built with Gemini. Drawn like a sunlit Edo woodblock and gold-leaf scroll, with five classic stages per cycle: two diving flocks of painted birds, two broods of kintsugi porcelain eggs that hatch into regrowing Great Solar Phoenixes, and The Sun-Ark, a descending pagoda fortress guarded by lacquer armour and a revolving bronze shield belt. Raise the Solar Ward force shield at the right moment, accompanied by synthesized pentatonic koto and taiko music.

Play SuncrestVersion 1.0.0

Sunspindle

Built with ChatGPT. Folded-metal swifts dive above a sunlit mountain lake. Break the eggs or take on the larger kites, then cut a path through the Solar Press and chase its spindle along the rail.

Play SunspindleVersion 1.0.0
